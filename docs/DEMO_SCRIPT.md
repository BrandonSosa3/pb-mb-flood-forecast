# Demo video: script and shot list (about 75 seconds)

**How to record**
- **Laptop:** QuickTime Player → File → New Screen Recording, browser at full screen, at
  https://pb-mb-flood-forecast.vercel.app.
- **Phone shots:** iPhone screen recording (Control Center).
- **Narration:** record your voice over it afterwards (iMovie), or talk while recording.
- **Footage:** your own boardwalk clip from Nov 14 is fine (blur faces). **No Surfline footage.**
- **Edit:** iMovie. Export 1080p, keep it under 90 seconds.

| # | Time | Shot | Say (roughly) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0–8 s | Your Nov 14 boardwalk clip (water over the Mission Beach wall), or a still | "This fall, El Niño pushed San Diego's sea level more than a foot above normal. On October 27 the tide gauge hit its highest hour on record." |
| 2 | 8–18 s | Phone: Home screen, scroll the area cards slowly | "I built a forecast that answers one question: is my area at risk this week? Plain-language status for Mission Beach, Pacific Beach and Crown Point, plus rain flooding for La Jolla too." |
| 3 | 18–28 s | Tap Mission Beach → area page; point at the tide, extra-water and surf tiles | "Under the hood: NOAA tide predictions, plus the extra water El Niño is adding, plus wave run-up from Scripps' CDIP wave forecasts, compared with 2024 USGS lidar of every street." |
| 4 | 28–43 s | Laptop: Map → 3D → **Play Oct 26**; let the water rise; end on 1,096 properties | "Here's October 26, when the sand berm on Bayside Walk broke. The model puts water at about eleven hundred properties, matching where news crews found flooded yards." |
| 5 | 43–52 s | Drag the slider; flip Berm holds / Berm fails | "Drag the water level to see which streets go first, with or without the berm, which turned out to be the biggest unknown." |
| 6 | 52–62 s | Switch to **Rain**; tap a Pacific Beach spot (e.g. Dawes St) | "Rain matters too. I mapped where rain water pools and tested it against City flood reports. In Pacific Beach, reports land at these spots 3.6 times more often than chance." |
| 7 | 62–72 s | How it works → scroll to the track record (show a "missed" card) | "Every forecast is logged before the event and scored after: 9 right, 3 close, 6 missed so far. The misses are public, and they're how the model improves." |
| 8 | 72–78 s | Home screen + URL on screen | "PB and MB Flood Forecast. Built by Brandon Sosa. Link below." |

**Tips**
- Before recording, close other tabs and hide bookmarks. Zoom the browser to 110% so the text reads on small screens.
- Do shot 4 a couple of times. The 3D view loads in a few seconds the first time.
- Captions help: most people watch muted. iMovie → Titles, or auto-captions when uploading to YouTube or LinkedIn.
- Upload unlisted to YouTube, then link it from the README and your application.
