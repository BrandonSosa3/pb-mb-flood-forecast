# Demo video: script and shot list (about 85–90 seconds)

**How to record**
- **Laptop:** QuickTime Player → File → New Screen Recording, browser at full screen, at
  https://pb-mb-flood-forecast.vercel.app.
- **Phone shots:** iPhone screen recording (Control Center).
- **Narration:** record your voice over it afterwards (iMovie), or talk while recording.
- **Footage:** your own boardwalk clip from Nov 14 (blur faces). **No Surfline footage.**
- **Edit:** iMovie. Export 1080p, keep it under 90 seconds.

| # | Time | Shot | Say |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0–15 s | Your Nov 14 Mission Beach boardwalk clip (water coming over the wall) | "This fall and into this winter, El Niño pushed San Diego's coastline to the limit. I saw things I've never seen in my lifetime here, like this, at the Mission Beach boardwalk where I check the surf every morning. A few weeks ago I also watched my friend's apartment flood from the Mission Bay side on a king tide." |
| 2 | 15–27 s | Phone: Home screen, scroll the area cards slowly, then the Rain section | "So I built a forecast that answers one question: is my area at risk of flooding? I focused on where I live: Mission Beach, Pacific Beach and La Jolla. It includes surf, tides, rain, the sand berms and a few other things." |
| 3 | 27–37 s | Tap Mission Beach → area page; point at the tide, extra-water and surf tiles | "Under the hood, I'm using NOAA tide predictions, plus the extra water El Niño is adding, plus wave run-up from Scripps' CDIP wave forecasts, compared with 2024 USGS lidar of every street." |
| 4 | 37–51 s | Laptop: Map → 3D → **Play Oct 26**; let the water rise; end on "1,096 properties" | "Here's October 26, when the sand berm on Bayside Walk broke. The model puts water at about eleven hundred properties, matching where news crews found flooded yards." |
| 5 | 51–59 s | Drag the slider; flip Berm holds / Berm fails | "Drag the water level to see which streets go first, with or without the berm, which turned out to be the biggest unknown." |
| 6 | 59–71 s | Switch to **Rain**; zoom into Pacific Beach; tap the **Figueroa Blvd** dot | "Rain matters too. I mapped where rain water pools and tested it against City flood reports. In Pacific Beach, reports land at these spots 3.6 times more often than chance. During Monday's storm, the City got flood reports right here." |
| 7 | 71–84 s | How it works → track record: open the **"Rain: Pacific Beach · automatic"** card, then scroll to a **Missed** card | "Every forecast is saved before the event and scored automatically the next day against rain gauges, tide gauges, City flood reports, NWS reports and local news, misses included. The misses are how the model improves." |
| 8 | 84–89 s | Home screen with the URL on screen | "PB and MB Flood Forecast. Built by Brandon Sosa. Link below." |

**Tips**
- Before recording, close other tabs and hide bookmarks. Zoom the browser to 110% so the text reads on small screens.
- Do shot 4 a couple of times. The 3D view loads in a few seconds the first time.
- If it runs over 90 seconds, trim shot 5 first: the slider is also visible in shot 4.
- Captions help: most people watch muted. iMovie → Titles, or auto-captions when uploading to YouTube or LinkedIn.
- Upload unlisted to YouTube, then link it from the README and your application.
